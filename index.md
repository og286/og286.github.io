Hello 👋
I am a clinical leader in the NHS with a strong interest in enabling staff to implement digital change in community settings. This website is an attempt to 'show my work' - most of the work being in the library.

I trained as a Speech and Language Therapist and have since worked in a wide variety of health care settings including Specialist Colleges, Community Learning Disability Services, Special Schools, Mainstream Schools, Neonatal and Pre-School Special Needs and Secure Mental Health settings. I am currently an operational lead in a community team, providing leadership to assistive technology, heart failure, cardiac rehab and parkinson's teams. 

I've got a wide variety of different interests in implementation of healthcare, with particular interests in:
- Implementation of digital healthcare including implementation (how do we implement with staff)
- Digital literacy and digital poverty 
- Healthcare in rural settings and coastal communities
- System working

I have a BSc. in Speech and Language Therapy, an MSc. in Healthcare Leadership and am about to start a PGDip in Digital Healthcare Leadership. I am also studying PRINCE2 which is so far outside of my comfort zone it is unreal. 

I post more regularly on [Twitter](https://www.twitter.com/JonRoustonSLT) - a mixture of daily nonsense and occasionally something useful. I keep a [library](https://publish.obsidian.md/jonrouston/_index) as a second brain as the first one is not always effective. 

You can see something like a [blog or updates here](https://rouston.co.uk/blog/).
